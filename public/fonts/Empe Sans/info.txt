aboutFont=Mechanically condensed grotesque typeface with italics that are extra slanted. Generally inspired by the 90s trend of using condensed type on various ephemera like album sleeves and magazines. The typeface name originates from Empedocles, who is best known for originating the cosmogonic theory of the four classical elements (air, fire, earth, water). Used for body text in the Czech culture magazine 'živel' issue 41.
