aboutFont=Ananas began as an idea to create a serif counterpart to a grotesque typeface. It reimagines sans-serif principles through the lens of classic Renaissance typefaces without aiming for historical revival. Instead, it explores their contemporary interpretation by merging traditional approaches with a constructed primitivism. Its simplified, dynamic forms are designed to be both distinctive and highly functional, offering strong readability across various sizes and long texts.
