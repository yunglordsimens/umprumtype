aboutFont=PyRoman is a sans serif font based on the CAD font Roman D (Duplex), originally from the early Hershey Fonts family of vector fonts (1967). The character's construction was adapted from humanist fonts such as Frutiger and similar fonts for use in typesetting. It is easily readable in text, where the geometric segments are visually lost; it also works in large headings, where its details instead stand out. The name PyRoman is a composite of the words Roman D and py*o.
