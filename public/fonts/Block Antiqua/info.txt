aboutFont=Block Antiqua is a new cut of the well-known Block font, which is considered in certain literature as the height of tastelessness that should rather not be shown. Yet the font was for a time quite popular to the point of overuse. The original designs have several cuts, ranging from condensed to bold cuts, I even found it in Block Fraktur. 
The main character of the typeface is retained: the rounded corners, the boldness, and most importantly the different pronation variations of each letter to make them ideal for block typesetting. Serifs break the classic rules of serif fonts in some cases.
