aboutFont=Asystematik draws inspiration from Oldřich Menhart’s handwritten Manuskript (1943). The project focused on analyzing and defining each of his letterforms to better understand their construction. The final design reflects my interpretation, shaped by technical insight into calligraphic techniques. With its organic forms, Asystematik pairs especially well with illustrations, complementing their expressive character.
