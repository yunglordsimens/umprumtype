aboutFont=B-ROT is a monospace typeface inspired by the aesthetics of typewriters, DIY publishing culture, and digital themes from the turn of the millennium. Rooted in the history of technical fonts, it also reflects current typographic needs. Suitable for both offline and online use, B-ROT combines strong character with functionality. Designed using a skeleton-based approach, it emphasizes rhythm and legibility, offering a headline presence with potential for use in text settings.
