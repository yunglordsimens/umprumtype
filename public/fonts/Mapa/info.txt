aboutFont=Urban planning can be a good tool in creating a font. When looking from above, the imagination can find many unexpected forms and connections of letters, which are directly formed from streets, highways or parks and at the same time contain the logic that exists in the structure of the city. Just change the angle of view and freely walk around the city map!
The font preserves the original forms that were found on the city map, as well as their alternative versions. At the same time, in the second version, all letters are combined with the help of an additional element-street, which creates the feeling of a continuous walk through the text.
