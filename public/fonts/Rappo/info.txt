aboutFont=The Rappo font is inspired by 19th century English circus posters. Excessively narrow Bodoni-type antiqua complements the Fraktur cut in equally tapered proportions. The font is not primarily intended for tlonger text. The Rappo Fraktur in this case serves more as an ornamental section of this typeface, as its character construction is based on the original fraktur. Therefore, it is relatively illegible to the general user.
