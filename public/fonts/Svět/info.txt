aboutFont=Svět was born from an attempt at digitalizing the text typeface found in the letterpressed Czech book series “Světová knihovna” (Books from the world) from 1906. It mainly tries to reinterpret the peculiar looks of its aged, lower-quality print through round and enlarged serifs and chamfered connections.
