description: My typeface resembles squiggles, originally drawn with no apparent
  purpose, yet it is possible to recognize individual letters as the basis for
  letterforms. Starting with “aimless curve pulling”, creating abstract
  letterforms out of visible “nothing”, the idea gained the potential to create
  more meaningful letterforms. I was also interested in making a script font —
  the end result is a sketch font that shows the meaning of connections through
  the chaos of aimless drawing. Letters change at the beginning and end of a
  word and when standing alone. A creative ode to chance, turning chaos into
  letters and uniting the abstract with the meaningful.
