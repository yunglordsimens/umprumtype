description: "Polia is inspired by the Renaissance typeface Poliphilo, cut by Francesco Griffo for Aldus Manutius's 1499 edition of Hypnerotomachia Poliphili. Polia is based on a 16 pt movable metal type print from our UMPRUM letterpress workshop. I kept some irregular lowercase widths and the monumental capitals, but stripped away historical and romantic details to find a contemporary book face. The Book cut is complete; an italic, likely based on Blado (1925), is in development."
