description: Polia is inspired by the Renaissance typeface Poliphilo, cut by
  Francesco Griffo for Aldus Manutius's 1499 edition of Hypnerotomachia
  Poliphili. Polia is based on a 16 pt movable metal type print from our UMPRUM
  letterpress workshop. I kept some irregular lowercase widths and the
  monumental capitals, but stripped away historical and romantic details to find
  a contemporary book face. The Book cut is complete; an italic, likely based on
  Blado (1925), is in development.
