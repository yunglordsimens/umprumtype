description: ULTRAFLEX grew out of combining traditional letterpress typefaces with
  AI. I built a tool that generates and updates a database of scanned fonts, then
  produces new variations or entirely new typefaces from it. The result blends
  chaotic yet familiar elements that recall the original type, with some letters
  deliberately out of place. Four glyph sets come out of one source — Original (the
  traced font), Closest copy, Experimental and Chaotic, the last one partly
  unreadable. Best for short phrases and headlines; individual glyphs can also be
  used on their own. Planned chaos as a homage to letterpress heritage.
