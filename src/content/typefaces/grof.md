description: "While examining frottages of various gravestones, one in particular stood out to me. It was monolinear and blocky, with sharp angles and straight lines — it almost looked futuristic. I was immediately inspired to revive this font and bring it into the digital age. The result is the typeface Geöf, which means “grave” in Icelandic. For my character set, I included not only English letters but also Icelandic special characters as well as Czech ones. This decision felt like a natural way to combine my Icelandic roots with the time I spent in Prague."
