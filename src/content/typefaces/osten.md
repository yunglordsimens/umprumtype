description: "The Osten typeface was created in 2022 as part of a semester assignment to design a text font for a new edition of Živel magazine. It is intended to ensure smooth readability for both short and long texts, particularly at smaller sizes. As this was the author’s first major experience designing a complete typeface, inspiration was drawn from established text fonts such as Lido and LL Bradford. A workshop at school with typographer Yevgeniy Anfalov, focused on drawing Cyrillic characters, also led to the development of a full character set for writing in various Slavic and Eastern European languages."
